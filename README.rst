What is PyFADe
------------------

PyFADe is a free Open Source library for Failure and Anomaly Detection on time series. The algorithms used here are based on classical time series models, such as Auto-Regressive Integrated Moving Average (ARIMA) models and on more recent advances based on distance and matrix profiles of signals.


The main features of PyFADe are:

  * Application of filtering and outlier removal techniques for signals
  * Fit of ARIMA and ARIMAX models on signals
  * Calculation of matrix profile of one-dimensional and multi-dimensional time series
  * Discrete wavelet decomposition of signals, and matrix profile calculation of decomposed signals
  * Calculation of K-dimensional profile of multi-dimensional time series
